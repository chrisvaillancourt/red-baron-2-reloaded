## Exhausted review retention

The parent counts at most **three independent review rounds per candidate/scope**:
initial review plus at most two correction/re-review cycles. Each round has independent
Standards and Spec review of an exact frozen revision; a corrected revision does not
reset the count. If round three retains blockers, preserve the branch, worktree,
retained patch and complete findings. Record a task-specific entry here with:

- Task/scope, three round numbers and exact reviewed revisions, full integration base.
- Owner, workspace/branch and concrete retained patch/evidence paths.
- Reason/unresolved findings and precise observable acceptance needed to clear them.
- **Concrete resumption trigger**, such as named owner decision, supplied missing
  fixture or upstream fix receipt; neither “later” nor another automatic retry suffices.

The parent marks STATUS **deferred**, links that entry, releases the implementation
track and does not publish the red candidate. A trigger requires evidence reconciliation
and an explicit newly bounded parent assignment before resuming. This is not an
unattended scheduler or another ready queue. No candidate is recorded as exhausted
without an actual third-round unresolved receipt.
